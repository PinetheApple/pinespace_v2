Most dark modes are a light theme with the lights turned off. Nocturne started dark.

## Surfaces, not colors

The palette is a ladder of elevation — each layer a touch lighter than the one beneath:

- `well` — the deepest recess
- `base` — the page
- `surface` — cards and panels
- `elevated` — hover and popovers

```css
--color-well: hsl(255 26% 13%);
--color-base: hsl(255 24% 17%);
--color-surface: hsl(255 22% 21%);
--color-elevated: hsl(255 20% 25%);
```

## One accent, used sparingly

Violet carries every interactive cue — links, focus rings, the active nav state.
Restraint is what makes the glow read as intentional.

<Callout variant="warning">
  Contrast still matters in the dark. Body text sits at ~80% lightness, never
  pure white, to keep long reads comfortable.
</Callout>
