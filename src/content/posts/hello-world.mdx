I rebuilt this site from scratch. The old one was fine — static, fast, forgettable.
This time I wanted motion to be a first-class concern, not a coat of paint.

# Main header
## Why rebuild

Three reasons drove it:

- **Motion as structure.** Animation should explain the page, not decorate it.
- **Content first.** Long-form writing and projects needed real layouts.
- **A system, not pages.** Every control lives in one design language — Nocturne.

<Callout>
  This whole site, including the component library, is open source.
</Callout>

The result is faster to extend and far more fun to work in. More on the motion
